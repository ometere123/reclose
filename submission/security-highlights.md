# Security highlights

Reclose narrows authority at multiple boundaries: evidence submitters do not choose effects, the semantic Judge cannot create policy authority, and the Kernel dispatches only policy-authorized typed actions. Policy expansion is timelocked; incident restrictions are reason-indexed; stale policy/Judge bindings and duplicate effects are checked; recovery uses explicit remediation and validation stages. Snapshot authority uses immutable references, independent fetches and content hashes.

These are implementation controls, not a claim of complete security. The 82-threat ledger remains incompletely reconciled, and critical/high live validation has not been completed. The isolated typed-address `decided` simulation passed, but the active deployed Kernel/Judge source predates that fix, preventing canonical E1 live containment proof until source parity is restored.
